Projects and regions · Existing website

Know the ground
before you invest.

Somaliland’s six regions sit on very different geology, from ancient crystalline basement in the west to sedimentary plateaus in the east.

Awdal

The north-western gateway, bordering Djibouti and Ethiopia, with Borama as its centre and the historic port of Zeila on the coast.

Geology: Coastal plain rising to highland basement rock, with mica schist hosting emerald-bearing pegmatites.

Maroodi Jeex

Home to Hargeisa, the capital and commercial hub, with the airport and the main road to the port of Berbera. It includes Gabiley.

Geology: Crystalline basement and the northern escarpment, with Precambrian granite and ultramafic bodies.

Sahil

The coastal region on the Gulf of Aden. Berbera port is the export gateway for the whole country and for Ethiopia.

Geology: Low coastal plain of recent sediments, the Guban Basin and gabbro hills around Sheikh.

Togdheer

A central region centred on Burao, on the route between the coast and the interior.

Geology: Basement outcrops grading into sedimentary plateau.

Sanaag

The north-eastern mountain region around Erigavo, with the high Cal Madow range and a long Gulf of Aden coastline.

Geology: Mountain basement in the west and a broad Mesozoic sedimentary belt in the east.

Sool

A broad eastern plateau region along the Ethiopian border, mainly pastoral land.

Geology: Eocene limestone and Mesozoic sandstone plateaus meeting the Haud plain. Very little modern work is published.

Recorded mineral occurrences

Borama emeralds

Emerald and beryl in pegmatites cutting dark mica schist. Borama is the centre of gemstone digging, which began in the 1990s.

Abdiqadir zone

Government survey block near the Ethiopia and Djibouti borders, targeting gold and base metals in basement rock.

Dhagax-kuure, Gabiley

Survey anomalies for gold, copper, lead and zinc; copper-molybdenum veins in Precambrian granite.

West of Hargeisa

Sapphire in mica-rich pegmatite and garnet along the Gabiley road; emerald reported around the capital.

Berbera coast

Coastal heavy-mineral sands and the Laalays gold zone. Commercial viability is not yet proven.

Daban Basin

Gypsum and anhydrite south-east of Berbera, with surface estimates above 100 million tonnes, and thin lignite seams.

Togdheer jade

A licensed nephrite jade mine and small-scale mining sites in Togdheer.

Laasa-Surad zone

Government block of about 288 square kilometres for gold and base metals in basement rock.

Dalan and Assilih tin

Cassiterite and tin-tantalum in quartz and pegmatite veins and stream gravels around Dalan, Majiyahan and Elayo.

Nugaal basin

Sedimentary basin in the east, part of the offshore and onshore oil and gas exploration blocks.

Darkainle complex

Alkaline complex north-east of Borama: apatite, niobium (pyrochlore) and molybdenum.

Daarburuq–Lafaruug pegmatite belt

Pegmatites along the Berbera–Hargeisa road with emerald, aquamarine, beryl, mica, columbite and piezoelectric quartz.

Zeila salt

Rock salt worked traditionally on the Awdal coast around the historic port of Zeila.

Company profile,
mission and vision.

“Exploring deep grounds. We strike treasure where hope and opportunity shine bright.”

Introduction

Green Gemstone Mining Company is a local mining and development company located in Hargeisa, Somaliland. Our primary focus is on the development of high-grade mineral deposits within the Rare Earth Metals, industrial metals, and gemstones sectors.

Green Gemstone Mining Company is proud of its diverse local workforce, encompassing employees from all regions of Somaliland. We are committed to safe and sustainable business practices that bring value to our employees, customers, shareholders, and local communities.

Company profile and strategy

Green Gemstone Mining Company is a diversified mining company executing two complementary business strategies focused on the mining & production of Industrial Precious & Non Precious Metals and Precious & Non Precious Gemstones of average to high purity concentrates and six excavation-ready development mining projects.

Vision

Processing and Export: Process rare earth and industrial metals in Somaliland and export the final metal at competitive international prices.

Employment: Provide employment opportunities to locals in rural areas, allowing them to mine minerals, which we then purchase and process.

Commitment: Committed to mining precious gemstones, rare earth metals, and industrial metals across Somaliland, aiming to expand our workforce to 1,000 employees within five years.

Mission

Short-Term Goals: Expand our team across Somaliland, connect with all miners and deposit holders of the minerals we plan on mining and processing.

Long-Term Goals: Establish Somaliland as a significant player in the global market for rare earth and industrial metals.
